About

Pommes de ciel publishes raw, interior writing: unfiltered, stream of consciousness, sometimes fiction. Not the carefully crafted story, but the thing as it arrives, edited only enough to keep it alive.

Each edition gathers five writers, one piece each. Editions are printed as small A6 books and published here in full.

The name turns pommes de terre, apples of the earth, toward the sky.
